FAQ

Questions advertisers ask us

Short answers from Paytm Ads on audiences, formats, data and getting started.

What is Paytm Ads?

Paytm Ads is a full-stack advertising network powered by purchase intelligence, built from what people pay for on Paytm. It runs ads in the Paytm app, on Paytm Soundboxes at shop counters, and beyond Paytm on partner publishers, social and search, streaming and connected TV, and the open web through DSPs. Branding builds reach with display, video and audio. Performance drives sales with deals formats, partner publishers and retargeting.

What is purchase intelligence?

Purchase intelligence is the audience signal Paytm Ads builds from what people pay for on Paytm and how they use the Paytm app. Paytm has more than 85 Mn monthly transacting users, who make more than 20 Bn transactions a month, and each payment is a verified record of spend. Paytm Ads uses these signals to choose who sees an ad, to show it at the moment people pay, and to measure results online and in stores.[9][1]

Who advertises on Paytm?

To date, more than 2,000 brands have advertised with Paytm Ads, and more than 500 advertised in FY’26 alone. They come from FMCG, e-commerce and retail, BFSI, auto, durables and real estate, telecom, media and entertainment, travel and tourism, education, and pharma and healthcare. Government and public-sector bodies have also empanelled Paytm Ads.[3][7]

How do I start advertising on Paytm?

To start advertising on Paytm, fill in the form at ads.paytm.com/contact or write to ads.marketing@paytm.com. Tell Paytm Ads who you want to reach and what you want to achieve. A specialist reads your brief and comes back with a plan for your audience, and nothing runs until you approve it.

Start planning your Paytm campaign

Tell us what you sell and who buys it, and a Paytm Ads specialist will plan your campaign across branding and performance.
