FAQ

Questions advertisers ask us

Short answers from Paytm Ads on audiences, formats, data and getting started.

What ad formats are available in the Paytm app?

Paytm Ads runs display, video and deals formats along the Paytm payment journey. Branding formats are the Ribbon, Masthead, Homepage Banner, In-processing Banner, Post Transaction Screen, Post Transaction Screen Takeover and Play-on-Pay video, plus Story Ads, Interstitials and Rich Media & DCO. Performance formats are Scratch Cards, Catalog Deals, Promoted Icons and gamified solutions such as Spin the Wheel, Jackpot and Smash & Win.

What is Soundbox advertising?

Soundbox advertising is audio advertising from Paytm Ads that plays your brand message on the Paytm Soundbox, the speaker that announces payments at shop counters across India. With more than 16 Mn Soundboxes at storefronts, it reaches shoppers as they pay, with 100% ad audibility for the merchant and the customer. You choose the stores by category, geography and language.[2][1]

What is the Post Transaction Screen?

The Post Transaction Screen is the screen the Paytm app shows the moment a payment completes, and Paytm Ads runs ads on it. It reaches people when their attention peaks. A Post Transaction Screen Takeover gives one brand all the ad space below the receipt for a full day.

Can I use Paytm audiences on YouTube or CTV?

Yes. Paytm Ads runs Paytm cohorts on social and search (Meta, Google), streaming (YouTube, JioHotstar, ZEE5 and more), connected TV and FAST (LG, Samsung, Xiaomi and more) and the open web through DSPs. The cohorts are built from spending and in-app behaviour, and Kantar measures brand lift.
