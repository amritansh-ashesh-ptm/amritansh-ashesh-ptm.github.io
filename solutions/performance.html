FAQ

Questions advertisers ask us

Short answers from Paytm Ads on audiences, formats, data and getting started.

How does the Paytm Audience Network work?

The Paytm Audience Network is the performance network of Paytm Ads. It finds high-intent shoppers by what they pay for and how they use the Paytm app, reaches them on Paytm and on partner publishers, and turns that intent into sales with deals formats such as Scratch Cards, rewards and retargeting. Partner publishers add brand-safe inventory at lower cost, with one team, one report and one bill.

How do Scratch Card ads work?

Scratch Card ads on Paytm reveal your offer the moment a payment completes. A Paytm user pays, wins a Scratch Card, scratches it to see your offer, redeems it and lands on your app or website. Paytm Ads builds Scratch Cards for redemption and repeat purchase, and pairs them with Catalog Deals on the Cashback & Offers page, where shoppers go looking for offers.

Can I retarget my own customers on Paytm?

Yes. With Paytm Ads audience retargeting, you share your customer list, Paytm matches it with Paytm users in a privacy-safe way, and those users see your ads across Paytm placements. You can re-engage app installers, lapsed buyers, cart abandoners, wishlisters and product viewers. Dynamic catalogue retargeting shows shoppers the exact product they viewed on another app, in a banner or Scratch Card.
