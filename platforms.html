How cohorts are built

Three steps turn payments into an audience you can buy

Paytm Ads builds every audience the same way, whether you pick a ready cohort or ask for a custom one.

  1. 01

    Start from spend

    A cohort starts with the people who recently paid in a category, in the Paytm app or a Paytm‑powered journey.

  2. 02

    Add in-app behaviour

    Layer how people use the Paytm app, with spend and category, to narrow the cohort to the buyers you want.

  3. 03

    Consented and aggregated

    Matching happens inside Paytm, and advertisers never receive anyone’s personal data.

FAQ

Questions advertisers ask us

Short answers from Paytm Ads on audiences, formats, data and getting started.

What data are Paytm audiences built from?

Paytm audiences are built from what people pay for and how they use the Paytm app. Paytm Ads draws on Paytm’s payments, financial services and commerce businesses, including Paytm UPI, Paytm for Business, Paytm Payment Gateway, Paytm Money, consumer and merchant loans, Paytm Insurance Broking, credit cards and Paytm Travel. Each cohort is made up of adult users who recently paid in a category through Paytm.

Is personal data shared with advertisers?

No. Paytm Ads works with consented, aggregated cohorts, and no personal data leaves Paytm. Advertisers choose audiences such as credit card bill payers or frequent travellers, and Paytm Ads reaches them on Paytm placements or on partner media. If you share your own customer list for retargeting, Paytm matches it with Paytm users in a privacy-safe way, and the matched users are reached without their personal data leaving Paytm.

Which audiences can I reach with Paytm Ads?

Paytm Ads lets you reach cohorts built from real payments, such as credit card bill payers, high-value electronics buyers, beauty and personal care buyers, fashion and apparel shoppers and frequent travellers. You can add in-app behaviour to build your own. Among Paytm’s monthly transacting users, 70% live beyond Tier 1 cities, 76% are NCCS A and B, and 84% are millennials or younger.[3]

How many people can Paytm Ads reach?

Paytm has more than 550 Mn registered users. In the app, Paytm Ads reaches more than 110 Mn monthly active users and more than 85 Mn monthly transacting users, who make more than 20 Bn transactions a month. In stores, Paytm has more than 50 Mn registered merchants and more than 16 Mn Soundboxes, which play audio ads to shoppers as they pay. Paytm Ads also runs the same cohorts on social, streaming and connected TV.[5][6][1]

Does Paytm Ads reach people beyond India’s big cities?

Yes. Paytm Ads reaches consumers across India, not only in the metros. Among Paytm’s monthly transacting users, ~30% live in Tier 1 cities, ~30% in Tier 2 cities and ~40% in Tier 3+ cities and towns, and 36% prefer a language other than English or Hindi. Paytm has at least one transacting user in 97%+ of India’s pincodes, so a campaign can reach small towns as well as large ones.[2][3]
