Glossary

Each Paytm Ads term, in one or two sentences

Definitions of the metrics, formats and audience terms we use, such as purchase intelligence, MTU and NCCS.

AOV

AOV (average order value) is the average amount a shopper spends in one order. Paytm deals formats aim to grow it by encouraging shoppers to add more to their cart.

Related: ROAS

Commerce media

Commerce media is advertising that uses a commerce or payments company’s first-party data to choose audiences and measure results, on the company’s own platforms or on other media. Paytm Ads is a commerce media business built on what people pay for on Paytm and how they use the Paytm app.

Related: Purchase intelligence, Paytm Audience Network

Dynamic catalogue retargeting

Dynamic catalogue retargeting is retargeting that shows a shopper the exact product they viewed on an advertiser’s or partner’s app. On Paytm, a retargeting partner passes the product and user signal to Paytm, and the same product reappears in a banner or Scratch Card.

Related: Retargeting

India 1

India 1 is the consuming class, the highest-spending tier in Blume Ventures’ Indus Valley Report segmentation of India by spending power. Its signature spends include investments, credit card bills, and beauty and personal care.

Related: India 2, India 3

India 2

India 2 is the aspirant class, the middle tier in Blume Ventures’ Indus Valley Report segmentation of India by spending power. Its signature spends include discount shopping, electronics on EMI and digital subscriptions.

Related: India 1, India 3

India 3

India 3 is the everyday spenders, the largest and lowest-spending tier in Blume Ventures’ Indus Valley Report segmentation of India by spending power. Its signature spends include prepaid recharges, kirana groceries and small-ticket payments.

Related: India 1, India 2

MAU

MAU (monthly active users) is the number of people who open the Paytm app in a month, whether or not they pay. It measures the reach of in-app placements, while MTU measures the people behind Paytm’s purchase signal.

Related: MTU

MTU

MTU (monthly transacting users) is the number of people who make at least one payment on Paytm in a month. Paytm reports it in its investor disclosures, and it is a stricter measure than MAU because every MTU has paid, where an MAU may only have opened the app.

Related: MAU

NCCS

NCCS (New Consumer Classification System) is the Indian market-research standard that grades households by the education of the chief earner and the consumer durables they own, from A, the most affluent, to E. Advertisers use NCCS A and B as shorthand for India’s higher-spending households.

Related: India 1, India 2

Paytm Audience Network

Paytm Audience Network is the Paytm Ads performance network that finds high-intent shoppers by what they pay for and how they use Paytm, and converts them with deals, rewards and retargeting on Paytm and partner publishers. On partner publishers, advertisers get one team, one report and one bill.

Related: Retargeting, Commerce media

Play-on-Pay

Play-on-Pay is a Paytm video ad format that plays a brand’s video, fully viewable and non-intrusive, while a payment completes in the Paytm app.

Related: Post Transaction Screen

Post Transaction Screen

Post Transaction Screen is the screen the Paytm app shows the moment a payment completes, when the user’s attention peaks. Ads on it reach people right after they pay, and a Post Transaction Screen Takeover gives one brand all the ad space below the receipt for a full day.

Related: Play-on-Pay

Purchase intelligence

Purchase intelligence is the audience signal Paytm Ads builds from what people pay for across Paytm’s businesses and how they use the Paytm app. It rests on verified records of spend, where most platforms can only infer intent.

Related: Commerce media, NCCS

Retargeting

Retargeting is advertising aimed at people who have already interacted with a brand, such as past buyers, app installers or cart abandoners. With Paytm Ads, an advertiser shares a customer list, Paytm matches it with Paytm users in a privacy-safe way, and those users see the ads across Paytm placements.

Related: Dynamic catalogue retargeting

ROAS

ROAS (return on ad spend) is the revenue a campaign earns for each rupee spent on its ads. Paytm deals formats aim to lift it by reaching shoppers who are already in buying mode.

Related: AOV

Soundbox advertising

Soundbox advertising is audio advertising that plays a brand message on the Paytm Soundbox, the speaker that announces payments at shop counters across India. Both the merchant and the customer hear it, and campaigns can target stores by category, geography and language.

Related: Purchase intelligence
