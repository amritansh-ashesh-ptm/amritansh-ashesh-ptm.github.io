FAQ

Questions advertisers ask us

Short answers from Paytm Ads on audiences, formats, data and getting started.

How much does it cost to advertise on Paytm?

The cost of advertising on Paytm depends on the format, the audience and how long the campaign runs, so Paytm Ads plans each budget around your brief. Tell us who you want to reach and what you want to achieve through the form at ads.paytm.com/contact, and a specialist will come back with a plan for your audience. Nothing runs until you approve it.

What happens after I contact Paytm Ads?

After you contact Paytm Ads, the team reads your brief and the goals you picked, and a specialist comes back with a plan for your audience. You approve the plan before anything runs. It helps to share your company, your goals, such as brand awareness, sales or reaching shoppers in stores, and a budget range if you have one.

Can agencies work with Paytm Ads?

Yes. Brands and agencies both work with Paytm Ads. Agencies can brief Paytm Ads through the contact form or at ads.marketing@paytm.com, and a specialist comes back with a plan. Paytm cohorts also run on the media agencies already plan, including social, streaming, connected TV and the open web.
